Attachments Guide

Weapon attachment stats, recoil data, mag sizes & weapon compatibility. Data from PDF v3.2.

Grips reduce recoil — horizontal (side-to-side kick) or vertical (gun climb). Most effective on fast-firing guns like Kettle, Stitcher, Bobcat.

Stocks affect dispersion recovery (crosshair shrink speed) and equip time. Choose based on your playstyle — aggressive or methodical.

Barrels and muzzle devices affect bullet velocity, noise, and shot dispersion.

Extended magazines increase ammo capacity. Each mag type only fits specific weapons. Mag III gives the largest capacity increase.
